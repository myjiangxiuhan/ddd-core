# Evidence rules

- Mark every extracted item as `confirmed`, `inferred`, `assumption`, or `pending`.
- Record the source for confirmed and inferred items; state the missing evidence for assumptions and pending items.
- Keep the evidence boundary explicit. Do not promote an inference or an assumption into a domain rule without supporting evidence.
- Surface conflicting rules, missing evidence, assumptions, and pending decisions instead of silently resolving them.
