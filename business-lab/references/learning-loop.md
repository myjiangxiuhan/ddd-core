# Business learning loop

Use this loop when the user does not know which rules exist yet. External projects expand the question space; they do not answer the user's business.

1. **Concrete case:** the user writes or tells one real scenario.
2. **Unknown map:** mark missing trigger, actor, authority, lifecycle, exception, value, time, and vocabulary. Do not fill them.
3. **External study:** research one primary source or pinned open-source project for one unknown.
4. **Compare:** show the external project's fact, our current evidence, the difference, and why it may not transfer.
5. **User choice:** the user accepts, rejects, or defers the candidate rule.
6. **Scenario test:** the user explains one concrete case that should pass or fail under the chosen rule.
7. **Record:** update the glossary, business cards, or decision register with the user's wording and source.
8. **Repeat:** choose the next unknown. Stop when the current mainline is good enough for the next explicit gate, not when every future possibility is known.

## Unknown map categories

- trigger and promise;
- actors, authority, and ownership;
- vocabulary and context;
- lifecycle and retention;
- permissions and visibility;
- time, expiry, waiting, and ordering;
- duplicates, idempotency, and retries;
- money, quota, entitlement, and obligations;
- audit, notifications, and external effects;
- failure, cancellation, restoration, and compensation.

The map is a learning aid. A missing category remains `pending`; it is not proof that the business has no such rule.
